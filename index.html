WFI–MRI Completed · MRI validated

MRI-compatible wide-field imaging

I built and integrated a wide-field optical imaging platform synchronized with whole-brain fMRI. It combines repeatable mechanical alignment with a Kinetix camera, six-channel illumination, NI-DAQ acquisition and MRI gating. The complete prototype was transferred from the optical bench to the scanner and validated end to end, including per-channel OME-TIFF storage and acquisition metadata.

WATER DELIVERY Completed · Calibrated

Calibrated water-delivery system

I designed and characterized a microcontroller-controlled water-delivery system for behavioral experiments. A 12 V solenoid valve dispenses through interchangeable capillaries, while a guided Arduino and balance workflow records repeated pulses, converts mass to volume, propagates measurement uncertainty and fits the volume–time response. The resulting calibration translates a target reward volume into the corresponding valve-open time and automatically produces traceable data, plots and a technical report.

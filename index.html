Wide-field MRI-compatible imaging

Completed · MRI validated

Integrated wide-field optical imaging platform synchronized with whole-brain fMRI. The MRI-compatible system combines repeatable mechanics, six-channel illumination, Kinetix camera timing, MRI gating, and NI-DAQ recording. The complete prototype was transferred from the optical bench to the scanner and validated end to end.

optics · electronics · mechanics · acquisition software

Calibrated water-delivery system

Completed · calibrated

Arduino-controlled water-delivery system for behavioral experiments, based on a 12 V solenoid valve and interchangeable capillaries. A balance-guided workflow converts repeated mass measurements into volume, propagates uncertainty, fits the volume–time response, and calculates the valve-open time required for a target reward volume.

fluidics · embedded control · calibration · data analysis

VR fresnel rig mounting

Mechanical mounting system for a three-screen visual arena for mice. Uses Fresnel lenses to shape the optical path and help keep brightness more uniform across screens.

mechanics
